OUR COMMITMENTS

Read the fine print. We wrote it to be read.

Your doctor stays in charge.

Human OS supports your doctor. It never replaces one. Whenever you need it, we produce a one-page summary of your data for your physician to review.

Your data belongs to you.

You own your EPLIMO and VieBiome results, and you can export everything. We will never sell your genetic data to insurers, employers, or anyone else.

A fair exit, in writing.

Change your mind before your samples are collected and you receive a full refund. No forms designed to be lost, no quiet deductions.

We stay accountable.

If you follow your plan for 12 weeks and the markers we agreed on together don’t improve, your programme continues free for another 12 weeks while we rework the plan with you.

And one honest boundary: we will not promise to predict your future or prevent any disease. Nobody can. What Human OS does is simpler and more useful: it reads what your body is actually doing, every day, and helps you act on it.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Asked, answered.

What does Human OS cost?

Nothing today. Joining the waiting list is free and carries no commitment. Programme details and pricing will be shared with the waiting list first, before any public announcement.

Is this a medical service?

No. Human OS is a wellness and lifestyle system. It supports your doctor and never replaces one. Face-scan and wearable readings are wellness indicators, not medical measurements, and any medical decision belongs with your physician.

What happens after I join the list?

You’ll receive a confirmation, and after that, launch updates as founding cohorts open. Cohorts unlock in list order and stay deliberately small, because lab capacity and coaching bandwidth are real limits we won’t pretend away.

Where is Human OS available?

We launch across India first, with the Gulf and other regions to follow. Join the list wherever you are. Your position is kept as regions open.

What do I need to get started?

A smartphone. The face scan uses your phone’s camera, and sample collection for the genetic and gut tests happens at home or at a Vieroots centre. A watch or ring helps but is optional.
