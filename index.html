Member journeys

What it looks like when your data finally talks.

Three illustrative journeys, drawn from the patterns we see across real Vieroots members. Names changed, no photos, and no outcome promised. Individual results vary.

Gut × Blood · Illustrative
Rakesh, 41 · Mumbai

Every annual check-up said 'fine'. He didn't feel fine.

Rakesh's blood work was normal, year after year, yet the bloating, the 3 pm crashes and the brain fog were real. His VIEBIOME results showed elevated yeast levels and low bacterial diversity, a picture of his gut that routine blood work isn't designed to give. His plan changed that week: different foods, a gut re-test on the calendar, and the full picture shared with his doctor.

Plate × Biosignal · Illustrative
Meera, 34 · Dubai

The pattern showed up in her data before she connected it herself.

Meera logged her meals by scanning her plate. Over weeks, Human OS correlated her heavier-carb lunches with her afternoon stress and energy readings, a pattern no single report had ever flagged. Dr. Vie rebuilt her lunch routine, and her afternoons stopped disappearing.

Trend × Time · Illustrative
Thomas, 52 · Bengaluru

Three years of 'normal' reports. One direction of travel.

Each of Thomas's yearly blood tests looked acceptable on its own. Read together as a trend, his metabolic markers had been drifting the wrong way for three years. Human OS surfaced the drift early, while it was still a lifestyle conversation, and his doctor got the full picture on one page.

Our commitments

Read the fine print. We wrote it to be read.

Your doctor stays in charge.

Human OS supports your doctor. It never replaces one. Whenever you need it, we produce a one-page summary of your data for your physician to review.

Your data belongs to you.

You own your EPLIMO and VIEBIOME results, and you can export everything. We will never sell your genetic data to insurers, employers, or anyone else.

A fair exit, in writing.

Change your mind before your samples are collected and you receive a full refund. No forms designed to be lost, no quiet deductions.

We stay accountable.

If you follow your plan for 12 weeks and the markers we agreed on together don't improve, your programme continues free for another 12 weeks while we rework the plan with you.

And one honest boundary: we will not promise to predict your future or prevent any disease. Nobody can. What Human OS does is simpler and more useful: it reads what your body is actually doing, every day, and helps you act on it.

Common questions

Asked, answered.

What does Human OS cost?
Nothing today. Joining the waiting list is free and carries no commitment. Programme details and pricing will be shared with the waiting list first, before any public announcement.
Is this a medical service?
No. Human OS is a wellness and lifestyle system. It supports your doctor and never replaces one. Face-scan and wearable readings are wellness indicators, not medical measurements, and any medical decision belongs with your physician. When useful, we prepare a one-page summary of your data for your doctor to review.
What happens after I join the list?
You'll receive a confirmation, and after that, launch updates as founding cohorts open. Cohorts unlock in list order and stay deliberately small, because lab capacity and coaching bandwidth are real limits we won't pretend away.
Where is Human OS available?
We launch across India first, with the Gulf and other regions to follow. Join the list wherever you are. Your position is kept as regions open.
What do I need to get started?
A smartphone. The face scan uses your phone's camera, and sample collection for the genetic and gut tests happens at home or at a Vieroots centre. A watch or ring helps but is optional.
Who sees my data?
You, and the coaching system working for you. You own your EPLIMO and VIEBIOME results and can export everything. We never sell your genetic data to insurers, employers, or anyone else.
